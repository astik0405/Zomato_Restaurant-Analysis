ZOMATO RESTAURANTS ANALYSIS – KEY INSIGHTS

1) Global Presence
- The dataset includes restaurants across 13 countries and 80 cities, showing wide global coverage.

2) Restaurant Count Summary
- Total Restaurants: 1097
- Total Cities: 80
- Total Countries: 13
- Total Cuisines: 372
This indicates a highly diverse restaurant and cuisine ecosystem.

3) Rating Trend
- The overall average rating is 2.91, suggesting mixed customer satisfaction levels.

4) Cuisine Popularity
- Some cuisines have the highest restaurant count, indicating strong demand and popularity.
- Cuisine-wise analysis helps identify the most served cuisines across countries.

5) Cuisine vs Rating Relationship
- Certain cuisines have high restaurant count but comparatively lower ratings.
- This helps identify cuisines where quality improvement may be needed.

6) Customer Engagement (Votes)
- Total votes are around 170K, indicating strong customer interaction and feedback activity.

7) Online Delivery Adoption
- A major portion of restaurants offer online delivery, showing strong adoption of digital food ordering.

8) Table Booking Availability
- Table booking feature is lower than online delivery, suggesting many restaurants focus more on quick-service delivery rather than reservations.

9) City-Wise Distribution
- The map view highlights city-wise restaurant concentration, helping identify business hotspots.

This insights file is based on the Power BI dashboard: Zomato Restaurants Analysis.
